# Using the Codeberg for contributing

I am no expert on this but I'm just saying you *can* use this...
Until anyone does though, I don't have much to say.

Right now though:

* [Codeberg issues][] would be equivalent to sending an email to me
  (maybe, but we will see)

  There are these three categories at the moment
  (I'll just copy this from Codeberg):

  - **Type/Bug** - You expected something else to happen but it didn't.
  - **Type/Docs** - About documentations of any kind.
  - **Type/Idea** - You wish for something to happen but it's not a thing yet.
  - **Type/Help** - You are wondering if something can be done but,
    after a fair amount of research, can't find an answer.
    Or maybe you just have a question you want to ask for clarity :)

* [Codeberg pull requests][] would be equivalent to
  sending `git-format-patch(1)` and `git-request-pull(1)`

... except I can add comments to your code if it's Codeberg so
it's probably nice to have it on the web :D Though that means
you do need an account on Codeberg to make one... I hope you can
figure that out since I can't write that much here :(
Suggestions are equally welcome, although please be practical
since there's just the one of me

There are no issue templates or bug report templates
(partly because Codeberg doesn't seem to support it =.=)
Just try your best to explain, and (if it's a bug) give
reproducible examples that help narrow down the problem,
I believe in you ;)

[Codeberg issues]: https://codeberg.org/rapidcow/perspective/issues
[Codeberg pull requests]: https://codeberg.org/rapidcow/perspective/pulls
